# Product Economics

## Problem Context

AI subscription spending is increasing rapidly across startups and small engineering teams.

Many teams subscribe to:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Cursor
- GitHub Copilot
- Gemini

without clear visibility into:
- usage efficiency
- overlapping subscriptions
- unnecessary premium plans
- unused seats

This creates avoidable operational spending.

---

# Product Goal

AI Spend Audit aims to help teams:
- reduce unnecessary AI expenses
- identify optimization opportunities
- improve AI tooling visibility
- estimate annual savings

through lightweight automated audits.

---

# Value Creation

The platform creates value by:
- reducing operational costs
- simplifying subscription analysis
- improving spending transparency
- accelerating AI tooling decisions

Even small monthly savings may compound significantly over annual operational budgets.

---

# Example Savings Scenario

Example:

| Tool | Current Spend | Recommended Spend |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| ChatGPT Team | $120 | $20 |
| Cursor Business | $200 | $20 |
| Claude Max | $150 | $30 |

Potential monthly savings:
- approximately $400

Potential annual savings:
- approximately $4800

---

# Operational Cost Structure

Current MVP infrastructure costs are low due to:
- Vercel deployment
- Firebase Firestore backend
- serverless architecture
- lightweight frontend rendering

This enables rapid MVP deployment without expensive backend infrastructure.

---

# Scalability Considerations

As usage grows, future operational costs may include:
- API usage fees
- authentication services
- analytics infrastructure
- database scaling
- AI model inference costs

---

# Potential Revenue Models

Possible monetization strategies:
- SaaS subscriptions
- premium audit reports
- enterprise dashboards
- recurring monitoring
- consultant tools
- team analytics

---

# MVP Economics Strategy

The current MVP prioritizes:
- low infrastructure cost
- rapid feature iteration
- user feedback collection
- product validation

instead of immediate monetization.

---

# Long-Term Economic Potential

As AI tooling adoption increases, demand for:
- AI cost visibility
- subscription optimization
- operational AI analytics

may continue growing, creating opportunities for SaaS-based spend management platforms.
