# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Product Positioning

AI Spend Audit is positioned as a lightweight SaaS platform that helps startups and small teams identify unnecessary AI subscription spending and optimize operational AI costs.

The platform targets teams rapidly adopting:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Cursor
- GitHub Copilot
- Gemini
- AI subscription tooling

without clear visibility into spend efficiency.

---

# Target Audience

Primary users:
- startups
- indie hackers
- engineering teams
- AI-first companies
- remote teams
- founders managing SaaS expenses

Secondary users:
- finance teams
- operations managers
- technical consultants

---

# Core Value Proposition

The platform helps teams:
- identify overspending
- consolidate overlapping tools
- optimize subscription plans
- estimate annual savings
- generate shareable audit reports

without requiring complex onboarding.

# MVP Strategy

The initial MVP focuses on:
- fast onboarding
- instant audit generation
- shareable reports
- simple UX
- rapid iteration

instead of advanced enterprise functionality.

---

# Success Metrics

Primary metrics:
- number of audits generated
- returning users
- report shares
- lead conversions
- average session duration

# Long-Term Vision

Long-term, the platform could evolve into:
- a centralized AI spend management platform
- AI SaaS analytics dashboard
- subscription optimization assistant
- operational AI budgeting platform
